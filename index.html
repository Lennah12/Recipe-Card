Berry & Fig Breakfast Bowl

Creamy yogurt, juicy berries and a crunch of toasted nuts.

Prep
10 minutes
Serves
2
Level
Easy

Ingredients

  • 300 g thick Greek yogurt
  • 100 g strawberries, halved
  • 80 g blueberries
  • 50 g blackberries
  • 2 ripe figs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp redcurrants
  • 30 g almonds, chopped
  • 30 g walnuts, broken
  • 2 tsp honey

Method

  1. Toast the almonds and walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes until fragrant. Let them cool.
  2. Spoon the yogurt into two bowls and smooth the surface.
  3. Arrange the strawberries, blueberries, blackberries, figs and redcurrants in sections on top.
  4. Scatter over the nuts and drizzle with honey. Serve right away.

Tip: Slice the figs just before serving so they keep their color.

Avocado, Cucumber & Tomato Sandwich

Fresh, crisp and finished with a squeeze of lemon.

Prep
10 minutes
Serves
2
Level
Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 slices wholegrain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 small cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 handful salad leaves
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Toast the bread until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with the lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the avocado generously over two slices of toast.
  4. Layer with cucumber, tomato, radishes and salad leaves. Season lightly.
  5. Top with the remaining toast, press gently and cut in half.

Tip: Swap the bread for rice cakes for a lighter, gluten-free version.
